Privacy policy

We collect nothing.

Last updated 14 October 2026

Free forever · No ads · No account · No tracking

The short version

Gita does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. Gita is free, and it will stay free.

What stays on your device

To remember your journey, Gita keeps these things on your phone or computer only:

  • your progress: lessons completed, verses understood and your daily streak
  • your choices from the first screens: why you came, your daily time and your reminder time
  • saved verses, key ideas you chose to remember, and journal reflections you write
  • reading settings such as language, text size and appearance

In the iPhone app this is stored in the app's private on-device storage. On the web it is stored in your browser's local storage. None of it is ever sent to us or anyone else. We cannot see it.

Reminders

If you turn on a daily reminder, the notification is scheduled by your own device. No server is involved, and you can turn it off in the app or in your device settings.

Listening to verses

"Listen" uses your device's built-in voices. The text is spoken on your device.

Deleting your data

Use You → Reset progress in the app, delete journal entries one by one, or simply delete the app (or clear site data in your browser). Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that removes it completely.

Children

Gita is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Sources

The Sanskrit text and transliteration come from the gita/gita dataset. English translations are by Swami Sivananda and Shri Purohit Swami. The Hindi translation, word-by-word meanings and chapter summaries are written for this app.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated first. Questions are welcome on the support page.
