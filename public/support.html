Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the app and begin.

Is it really free?
Yes. Every verse, lesson and feature is free, with no ads and no subscriptions, now and later.

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too. We can't restore it, because we never had it.

Can I use it without internet?
Yes. All 700 verses and 140 lessons are inside the app.

How do I change the reminder or language?
Go to You in the app. You'll find daily time, reminder, language, translation, text size and dark mode there.

Why aren't the verse numbers the same as my book?
Some editions count Chapter 13 as 34 verses and others as 35 (Arjuna's opening question). Gita follows the 35-verse numbering, for 701 verses in all.
